Construction technology in practice

Field methods, connected
to the project record.

Explore how visual capture technologies support progress reviews, coordination, inspections and handover. Each method becomes more useful when its imagery stays connected to the place, date and project it documents.

01

Drone mapping & photogrammetry

Use dated aerial imagery and mapped flight paths to review site coverage, compare capture sessions and give teams a shared view of progress.

02

Reality capture & 3D scanning

Connect spatial scans and walkthrough captures with the project areas they describe, helping teams review conditions and keep specialist deliverables organized.

03

Visual progress reporting

Bring field photos and flight footage into a dated project history so owners and project teams can discuss visible changes with consistent context.

04

Inspection & handover documentation

Keep inspection media and supporting documents alongside the relevant capture date and site area, preserving a clearer record through handover.
