Our goal

Make the project
easier to understand.

Construction produces valuable visual information every day. Capture IV gives that information a structured home, with the project, date and location context people need to find it, understand it and share it responsibly.

Our vision

A connected record.
A shared perspective.

We envision a project record that brings aerial footage, site photography, documents and specialist capture deliverables together—helping teams communicate clearly from the first capture to final handover while keeping access under project-team control.

Controlled access

Use project access codes, read-only viewing and team-selected sections to share information with the right audience.

Secure collaboration

Share a project link and code deliberately, and keep client viewing separate from project editing workflows.

Documented context

Keep files and visual records tied to their project, capture date and site area so shared information remains understandable.
