Capture & processing workflows

See the site.
Understand the detail.

Specialist capture and processing workflows that inform the Capture IV platform roadmap.

Terrestrial LiDAR / 3D Laser Scanners

High-density tripod scanning for detailed structural baselines. Millimeter-level accuracy depends on the instrument, site conditions and survey controls.

Drone / UAV Photogrammetry Fleets

Commercial aerial capture workflows for earthwork volumes, topographic maps and large-site overviews, using appropriate processing and survey controls.

360° Construction Walkthrough Cameras

Field-crew captures for repeated structural and interior visual progress documentation.

Pix4D & Processing Engines

Reference processed deliverables from cloud or desktop photogrammetry tools that turn aerial photographs into maps and measurable models.

OpenSpace / HoloBuilder

Plan for deliverables from documentation tools that associate 360° imagery with project floor plans. Third-party accounts and supported exports are separate.

Subsurface scanning & underground mapping hardware

Keep the unseen
part of the record.

Organize specialist investigation and inspection deliverables alongside the wider construction record.

High-Frequency Concrete GPR Scanners

Handheld radar workflows for investigating shallow rebar, post-tension cables and conduits within concrete slabs.

Cart-Based Multi-Channel Utility GPR

Wheeled ground-penetrating radar workflows for investigating outdoor utilities and voids. Detection depth varies with ground conditions and the target.

Electromagnetic (EM) Locators

Induction-based locating workflows for tracing conductive utilities and energized cables where suitable signal conditions exist.

Robotic CCTV Pipeline Crawlers

Remote-controlled cameras for collecting visual inspection records inside stormwater and sewer pipelines.

SLAM Mobile Underground LiDAR

GPS-denied handheld or vehicle-mounted scanning for mapping tunnels, mines, vaults and other enclosed spaces.

These specialist workflows describe the planned scope of deliverable support and services. Hardware supply, scanning services, automated measurements and named software integrations are not currently included in the live portal. Availability and supported formats will be confirmed for each project. Third-party names describe the workflows, not an affiliation or existing integration.
